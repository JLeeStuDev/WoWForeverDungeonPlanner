How status works. No one gets kill XP from an enemy when anyone in the party is 13 or more levels above it (Blizzard hotfix, Nov 14 2026). Gray uses the Classic formula against your highest member. "Underleveled" means someone is more than 4 levels below the best level.

Data. For the 9 new dungeons, enemy ranges are Blizzard's announced ranges plus recorded boss levels. For the original 20, enemy levels are the original Classic values; Forever has changed these dungeons, so treat them as ±1–2. Best level = a few levels above the lowest enemy, so trash stays green while the last bosses are still beatable.
